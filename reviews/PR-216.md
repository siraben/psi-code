## Author intent

> - snapshot the editor draft when entering queued-message navigation
- on Escape/abort, restore each queued message exactly once followed by the pre-navigation draft
- discard uncommitted edits to a queued preview instead of duplicating them into the restored editor
- clear navigation state atomically and restore the draft after committing or consuming a preview
- honor configured queue-navigation
